Build an experiment from scratch

Not a template with the stimuli swapped out - an experiment that did not exist before. Work down the seven steps: who you are, what participants see, what they answer, the trials themselves, the timing, and - if you want it - a description of the design in the ABCD framework that the platform will check against what you actually built. At the end you get a link you can send to participants, and the results come back under "Get My Data" like any other experiment here.

Prefer to draw the trial rather than type millisecond values? Build it on the timeline instead → - same builder, the timing comes from a timeline you drag.
